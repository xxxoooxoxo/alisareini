Uyghur phrases which show the grammatical breakdown of the language.

This is inspired by the work of Tim Ferriss and his advice for learning languages affectively.

It is important to note that the Uyghur language follows the Subject-Object-Verb (SOV) word order, which is different from the Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) word order in English.

## Basic Statement
**English:** The apple is red.

**Uyghur:** Alma qizil.

**Literal:** Apple red.

## Possession
**English:** It is John's apple.

**Uyghur:** U John'ning almisi.

**Literal:** It John-of apple-his.

## Simple Giving (First Person)
**English:** I give John the apple.

**Uyghur:** Men John'ge alma barimen.

**Literal:** I John-to apple give-I.

## Group Giving (First Person Plural)
**English:** We give him the apple.

**Uyghur:** Biz uning'gha alma berimiz.

**Literal:** We him-to apple give-we.

## Third Person Giving (Named Recipient)
**English:** He gives it to John.

**Uyghur:** U uni John'ge beridu.

**Literal:** He it John-to gives.

## Third Person Giving (Pronoun)
**English:** She gives it to him.

**Uyghur:** U uni uning'gha beridu.

**Literal:** She it him-to gives.
